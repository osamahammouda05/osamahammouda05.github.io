Volunteer project, April 2026

905 Gunners Charity Tournament

Planned and ran a 10-team charity tournament end to end, raising $1,000+ for Human Appeal

Goal

Raise money for Human Appeal's orphan support by running a community soccer tournament with no budget, staff or venue lined up.

What I did
  • Co-led planning with a teammate through near-weekly check-ins to track tasks, deadlines and progress
  • Booked the venue at Mississauga Entertainment Sports Complex and managed all communication with the facility
  • Coordinated the partnership and donations with Human Appeal
  • Secured prize sponsors: Erin Mills Soccer Club, Synergy Health & Sports Rehab and Shields Auto Centre
  • Promoted the event on Instagram and TikTok
Result

10 teams and about 120–140 players took part on April 5, 2026, raising just over $1,000.

What I learned

Running a project with volunteers means keeping everyone accountable without authority. Regular check-ins and clear owners for each task kept us on schedule.
