Clinic wait-time reduction

I compared three ways to cut a children's clinic's visit times by 20% and recommended better scheduling and staffing as the lowest-cost, lowest-risk option.

Problem
A children's orthopedic clinic needed to cut visit times by 20%.
What I did
Compared three options (a dedicated X-ray machine, better scheduling and staffing, longer clinic hours) and calculated each one's effect on wait times and cost.
Result
Recommended better scheduling and staffing: no new equipment or hires, and the lowest risk.
