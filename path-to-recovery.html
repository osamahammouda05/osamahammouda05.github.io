Side project, in development

Path to Recovery

Leading a four-person team building a symptom-guidance app for physiotherapy patients

Illustration
Problem

People with pain or injuries often don't know whether to rest, stretch or see someone, and most symptom apps are either too generic or not backed by clinicians.

What I do
  • Assembled and lead a team of a physiotherapist, a massage therapist and a developer
  • Defined requirements through competitor research and gap analysis
  • Designed the symptom-based questioning flow with the clinicians
  • Run meetings, set deadlines and track bi-weekly feedback to set priorities
  • Wrote the in-app consent and endorsement copy
Status

In development. The physiotherapist and massage therapist have both endorsed the content.

What I'm learning

Turning expert knowledge into a clear user flow takes as much requirements work as a BI project does. I translate what clinicians know into steps a developer can build.
