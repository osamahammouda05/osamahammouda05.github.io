Dashboard, Bunzl Canada, Sep 2025

Sales growth analysis

Two periods side by side, with real growth separated from inflation.

Sample data

Tap the visual to view it larger

Context
Leadership wanted to compare sales and profit between any two time periods, and to see how much growth was real versus just higher prices.
Action
I built one dashboard that puts two chosen periods side by side.
  • Pick any two periods to compare
  • Choose how to break the numbers down (by region, customer type, sales rep and more) from a simple list
  • See units sold, sales, cost, profit and the share of house-brand products for both periods
  • Charts show how sales and profit move month to month
Result
Leaders can answer their own comparison questions from one page, instead of asking for a new report each time.
Learning
One flexible page beats ten fixed ones, but only if the calculations work no matter which breakdown someone picks. Building them that way took more thought up front and saved rework later.
