Open order audit reports

One page per order type: what can ship and what needs follow-up.

Sample data

Tap the visual to view it larger

Context
Customer service needed to see which open orders could ship from stock, which were waiting on product, and which needed someone to follow up, across several kinds of orders.
Action
A report with one page for each type of order, each showing just what's needed to make a decision.
  • All open orders by sales rep, customer and delivery location
  • Pick-up orders, color-coded from "no stock" to "ready", with expected dates and staff notes
  • Orders that must ship complete, showing how much of each order is ready
  • Special orders matched to the supplier's purchase order, with a clear "action required" flag
Result
The team has one place to review each type of order, with the stock situation and next step on the same line.
Learning
Different questions need different views. Separate, focused pages worked much better than one huge table with every column.
